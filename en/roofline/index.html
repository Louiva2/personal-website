Finding the Edge

How fast a card can run has an upper bound that can be worked out on paper first. Here I work it out, then measure one model at a time, to see how far the measurement falls short of the edge and where the gap comes from.

Where it starts

    Only then come the models, starting with the one whose accounts are cleanest: a single card, dense, fp16. Each model after that adds just one term to the bound: quantization, long context, MoE, several cards.
